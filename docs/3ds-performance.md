# 3DS (Azahar) on the PS4: where the speed is decided

For the maintainers and the agents who will tune the 3DS core. Written 2026-10-19 against Azahar 2126.2
(unified tarball, GIT-COMMIT 219d743a, in WSL at `/root/src-research/azahar` and in the scratchpad's
`dl-azahar-unified\azahar-unified-source-2126.2`; `externals/dynarmic` is the azahar-emu fork) and EmuSuNi 00.56. `scripts/build-3ds-core.sh` did not exist yet when this was written: the
phase-1 porter is making it. Tags: [measured] done on this PC, [confirmed] read in the source, [estimate]
derived or guessed. Line numbers are of the files named; the EmuSuNi ones are of 00.56.

## Resumen

El 3DS es el port más pesado hasta ahora: Azahar emula los 2 (3DS) o 4 (New 3DS) núcleos ARM11 **uno tras
otro en un solo hilo del PS4** (`core.cpp:274-307`), y un núcleo Jaguar a 1,6 GHz tiene un 30 % de la
velocidad mononúcleo que Azahar pide como mínimo [estimación]. Ahí se decide casi todo; el resto son
sobresaltos, no velocidad media. Los cuatro sitios que importan, por orden:

1. **El JIT de CPU (dynarmic)**: obligatorio (el intérprete es 8-12x más lento [medido]); se afina con Old 3DS
   (2 JITs), con el % de reloj de CPU y, quizá, con PGO como en la DS. Pide memoria ejecutable como los demás.
2. **Los shaders y pipelines Vulkan**: Azahar compila GLSL/SPIR-V y pipelines al vuelo; en el PS4 cada
   pipeline costó 5-19 ms, y 60-140 ms en el arranque en frío de God of War (PSP, 00.47). Cachés en disco,
   compilación asíncrona e hilos a una sola prioridad.
3. **La traducción PICA -> Vulkan por frame**: vertex shaders en la GPU (`use_hw_shader`), multiplicación
   exacta apagada, 1x, sin filtros de textura; medir las lecturas de la GPU que paran la emulación.
4. **El coste fijo y la presentación**: VulkanWorker, hilo de fences, `set_image`; 0,35-0,6 ms/frame en el PC
   [medido] = 2-6 ms en el PS4 [estimación]. Plan: una línea de tiempos cada ~10 s como la de la DS y la PSP,
   probar primero homebrew en el PC y los juegos del dueño en el PS4, y si no llega: Old 3DS, reloj al 50-75 %,
   ojo derecho apagado, y marcar como no jugables los New 3DS y los 3D pesados que queden por debajo del 50 %.

## 3. The critical parts, ranked by expected effect on frame time

### 3.1 The ARM11 emulation: dynarmic on one thread (the ceiling)
- **What**: 2 (Old 3DS) or 4 (New 3DS) `ARM_Dynarmic` instances (`core/core.cpp:453-456, 596-613`), one A32 JIT per core and per page table (`arm_dynarmic.cpp:334-352, 355-374`), run in slices of at most `MAX_SLICE_LENGTH` = 268111856/234 cycles (`core_timing.h:37, 184`) by `System::RunLoop`; memory through the page table; exclusive monitor `DynarmicExclusiveMonitor` (`core/arm/exclusive_monitor.cpp:19-20`).
- **Why it matters**: commercial games saturate the ARM11, and a PS4 core is 30% of Azahar's minimum [estimate]. Homebrew that waits for VBlank cost 0.35-0.6 ms a frame on this PC with the JIT and 4.4-6.5 ms with the interpreter [measured, `research-3ds-port.md` 1.3]: the interpreter is out, and `emusuni_make_executable` failing must refuse the game, not fall back.
- **Knobs**: `citra_use_cpu_jit` (on); `citra_is_new_3ds` (`Old 3DS`: 2 JITs, 2 cores per slice; N3DS-only games then refuse to load, `core.cpp:427`); `citra_cpu_clock_percentage` 25-400 (default 100): at 50% each executed tick counts double against the slice (`core_timing.cpp:179`), so the game gets half the CPU time per frame and must cope; dynarmic's `optimizations` are left at `all_safe_optimizations` (`config.h:138`), its `Unsafe_*` flags (`interface/optimization_flags.h:38-51`: `UnfuseFMA`, `ReducedErrorFP`, `InaccurateNaN`, `IgnoreStandardFPCRValue`, `IgnoreGlobalMonitor`) are untried; `code_cache_size` (`config.h:239`) 16-32 MiB per JIT from the pool, `useProtect()` false (`block_of_code.cpp:75, 110-112`: W^X is only compiled with `DYNARMIC_ENABLE_NO_EXECUTE_SUPPORT`).
- **How to measure**: TSC around `ARM_Dynarmic::Run` (`arm_dynarmic.cpp:192`, already `MICROPROFILE_SCOPE(ARM_Jit)`), per emulated core; blocks compiled per 10 s (a counter in `BlockOfCode`/`A32::Jit`); the idle share (`Timer::Idle`) to see whether the game or the emulator is the bottleneck.
- **First experiment**: the same game on the PS4 at 100% and 50% clock, New and Old 3DS, read off the timing line: if ARM time barely moves at 50%, the cost is HLE/GPU, not the JIT.

### 3.2 Shader and pipeline compilation (the stutter)
- **What**: `ShaderDiskCache::UseProgrammableVertexShader/UseFixedGeometryShader/UseFragmentShader` queue compiles on `shader_workers` (`vk_shader_disk_cache.cpp:81-235`); `PipelineCache::BindPipeline` builds or waits (`vk_pipeline_cache.cpp:366-374`, `vk_graphics_pipeline.cpp:84-109`); the driver cache and the SPIR-V cache live under the save dir and are loaded by `LoadDefaultDiskResources` at game start (`citra_libretro.cpp:411-414`, `gpu.cpp:519-521`).
- **Why**: on the PS4's RADV a pipeline took 5-19 ms warm, and 60-140 ms each for the 115 God of War rebuilt at every start before its driver cache was kept (PSP, 00.47): that cold-start case is Azahar's first run of a title and what sizes the cache; a ParaLLEl-RDP shader up to 0.7 s (00.22); Jaguar also runs glslang's GLSL front end slowly [estimate].
- **Knobs**: `citra_use_disk_shader_cache` (on), `async_shader_compilation` (patch, see table), `disable_spirv_optimizer` (default true: no SPIRV-Tools pass, good), `spirv_shader_gen` (true: most fragment shaders are emitted as SPIR-V and skip glslang; a config for which `UsesSpirvIncompatibleConfig()` is set still goes GLSL -> glslang, `vk_shader_disk_cache.cpp:162-170`), worker count and priority (hook).
- **Measure**: count and ms of `CompileGLSL`, `CompileSPV`, `GraphicsPipeline::Build`, time blocked in `BindPipeline(wait_built=true)`, cache hits at start (the PSP line: "compiled N pipelines (ms) and M shaders (ms)").
- **First experiment**: a game's first 60 s cold, then warm; the warm run should show zero compiles.

### 3.3 PICA to Vulkan per draw
- **What**: `PicaCore::DrawArrays` tries `rasterizer->AccelerateDrawBatch` (hardware vertex shaders) and otherwise runs the vertex shader on the CPU per vertex (`video_core/pica/pica_core.cpp:1068-1090`, `LoadVertices`) through the shader JIT (`shader_jit_x64_compiler.cpp:1035, 1103`; interpreter if `use_shader_jit` is off, `shader.cpp:21`); accurate multiplication is set per title (`gpu.cpp:378-396`, `SetAccurateMul`); textures go through `RasterizerCache` (`rasterizer_cache/rasterizer_cache.h`) with optional GPU filters (`vk_blit_helper.cpp:634`).
- **Why**: everything the PICA200 does is translated on core 2 before the GPU sees it; the Jaguar core pays per draw and per texture upload/download. The GPU (GCN) should be far from its limit at 400x240 [estimate], and the PS4's only GPU-side numbers say where that ends: the PSP at 4x + MSAA ran at 85-90% with the drawing waiting 6-7 ms a frame for the GPU (GPU busy 55-63%, 00.46), and Ghost of Sparta's first-frame readbacks drained the GPU at 29-97 ms each (00.47). So `citra_resolution_factor` above 1 and the GPU texture filters are the first suspects once the GPU shows up, and texture downloads must be counted.
- **Knobs**: `citra_use_hw_shader` (on), `citra_use_shader_jit` (on, for the draws hardware can't take), `citra_shaders_accurate_mul` (**off** on the PS4; the core defaults it on, `core_settings.cpp:331-344`), `citra_resolution_factor` 1 (above it the PSP's 4x numbers apply), `citra_texture_filter` none, `citra_texture_sampling` GameControlled, custom/dump textures off, geometry shaders only if `geometryShader` is enabled on the app's device (it is not today: `src/Display.cpp:303-308` wants dualSrcBlend, sampleRateShading, logicOp, depthClamp, clip/cull distance, and the `GRANITE[]` list `:343-356` has no geometryShader; `research-3ds.md`, integration).
- **Measure**: draws per frame, accelerated vs software, vertices through the shader JIT, shader JIT compiles, surfaces uploaded/downloaded (bytes), `Scheduler::Finish` waits (readbacks; the PSP's 29-97 ms per first-frame download is the reference).
- **First experiment**: accurate mul on vs off and hw shaders on vs off on one game, one line each.

### 3.4 The fixed cost and the present path
- **What**: per VBlank `GPU::VBlankCallback` -> `RendererVulkan::SwapBuffers` -> `DrawScreens` -> `PresentWindow::Present` -> `set_image` (`gpu.cpp:488-498`, `renderer_vulkan.cpp:1126-1150`, `libretro_vk.cpp:689-707`); `GetRenderFrame` calls `wait_sync_index`/`get_sync_index` (`libretro_vk.cpp:643-653`); every submit under `lock_queue` with a fence per submit and a wait thread (`MasterSemaphoreLibRetro::SubmitWork/WaitThread`, `:777-833`); HLE DSP every `audio_frame_ticks` = 160 x 4096 x 2 ARM11 cycles (~3.4 ticks a frame, `hle.cpp:49, 445-475`).
- **Why**: 0.35-0.6 ms a frame on this PC, 2-6 ms on the PS4 [estimate at 6-10x]: a third of the budget before the game runs an instruction.
- **Knobs**: `citra_audio_emulation` hle (lle/lle_multithread are slower); `use_skip_duplicate_frames` (`renderer_vulkan.cpp:239`, off) skips presenting an unchanged frame; the PSP/DC present patch (never `wait_sync_index`, a ring of the core's own images, `set_image` at the end of `retro_run`, `device.waitIdle()` under the lock).
- **Measure**: `SwapBuffers` time, time inside `lock_queue`, fence waits, HLE audio tick time (`DspHle::Impl::Tick`), the libretro `audio_batch` time.

### 3.5 Memory behaviour (hitches, not frame time)
FCRAM in a direct-memory block; the save state streamed; the JIT pools' size logged; `hardware_concurrency()` and the thread stacks checked. Also `std::random_device` in `core_timing.cpp:42` (untested on the PS4 kernel).

## 4. Measurement plan

**The log line** (every ~10 s from `retro_run`, TSC counters summed from any thread as `Common/EmuSuNiTiming.h` in `build-psp-core.sh:1383-1460`, printed as `build-psp-core.sh:1795-1842` and `build-nds-core.sh:969-985` do). Proposed content, one `3ds:` line plus a detail line:
`3ds: N frames in S s (F/s); per frame: retro_run X ms = ARM11 A (core0 a0, core1 a1, idle i; B blocks compiled) + kernel/HLE K + GPU commands G (D draws: H accelerated, V software vertices, J shader JIT compiles) + audio HLE U + SwapBuffers/present P (waiting for the worker W, inside lock_queue L) + the rest R; worker: recording Rw ms, waits for pipelines Wp (n), compiled Np pipelines (ms) and Ns shaders (ms); textures: Tu uploaded, Td downloaded (Finish waits F); FCRAM block, JIT pool used/size`. Azahar already has hooks to reuse: `perf_stats->BeginGPUProcessing/EndGPUProcessing` around `gpu.Execute` (`hle/service/gsp/gsp_gpu.cpp:681-683`), `StartSwap` (`renderer_vulkan.cpp:1127`), `MICROPROFILE_SCOPE(ARM_Jit)`. The app's own `pacing:` lines (`src/Emulator.cpp:2186-2192`, `:3085`: "the core takes X ms a frame: one frame a pass") give retro_run p50/p95/p99 and the heavy transitions; `core_avg` is a 0.9/0.1 moving average of `s_core->run()` (`:3150-3156`).

**PC to PS4 factor**: 6x-10x on CPU-bound time, from the DS calibration (Mario Kart DS 2.63 ms on the PC vs 15-17 ms on the PS4 in 00.38, ~6x; 1.84 ms on the PC after 00.40 vs the 00.55 PS4 log's low lines, 82-88% speed with the core at 16-18 ms a frame (scratchpad `ps4-0055\emusuni.log`, `speed:` lines; the same session also shows 97-100%), ~10x at worst; the CHANGELOG estimated ~9.5x: "1.84 -> ~17.5 ms", 00.40). Caveats: (a) the PC Azahar runs were on lavapipe (software Vulkan): only the main thread's CPU time is scaled, the PS4 GPU/driver time is unknown; (b) pipeline and shader compile times do not scale with it (RADV on the PS4: 5-19 ms per pipeline); (c) dynarmic and Azahar pick code paths by host features (no AVX2/BMI2/FMA on Jaguar), so the JIT's output differs from the PC's; (d) homebrew measures the fixed cost, not the JIT's throughput: no game number exists for Jaguar.

**Harness**: `scratchpad\wsl\az\azfront.cpp` (headless libretro host: wall, main-thread CPU and process CPU per `retro_run`, scripted input, PNG dumps; built by `az-front-build.sh`, runs `az-run1..6.sh`), the same shape as the Dreamcast's `wsl\dc\dcfront.cpp` (wrapped malloc for fresh-address checks) and the project's `local/herramientas/prueba-*` tests. Add: two games loaded in one process; a run with every option at its PS4 value; the timing line parsed into a table.

**Benchmarks**: PC (free homebrew only): Super Haxagon 3.9.1 (`.3dsx`), Craftus Reloaded 0.3 (walking, chunk generation spikes), plus one 3D homebrew that keeps the ARM11 busy (to be found). PS4: the owner's own decrypted games, one per class of `research-3ds-port.md` 1.4 (light 2D / mid 3D / heavy 3D), each measured cold and warm (shader caches), at 100% and at 50-75% clock, New and Old 3DS, read from the log the owner sends.

## 5. Fallbacks if full speed is out of reach

| Fallback | Mechanism | Cost / risk |
|---|---|---|
| EmuSuNi's heavy mode (already there) | One frame a pass, every frame shown, the sound slowed in step (`Emulator.cpp:3079-3110`) | Slow motion with correct pitch; the game's own 30 fps logic unaffected |
| CPU clock below 100% | `citra_cpu_clock_percentage` 50-75: each executed tick counts 100/percentage against the slice (`core_timing.cpp:46-49, 179`), so the game gets fewer ARM11 cycles a frame | Two claims disagree and the experiment of 3.1 tests the first: the Qt tooltip (via the research) says lower values speed the emulator up and can freeze games; the libretro option's own text says higher values "may improve performance", lower ones "help with games that run too fast" (`core_settings.cpp:165-168`). Variable-timestep games stutter, fixed-step ones may drop to 30 fps by themselves [estimate]; no effect on idle homebrew [measured] |
| Right eye off | `disable_right_eye_render` (patch; default off, `settings.h:634`): `RightEyeDisabler` blocks the second top-screen command list and display transfer (`video_core/right_eye_disabler.cpp:11-56`) | "Up to 50%" in stereo-rendering games (Azahar 2120 notes, research); flicker in some |
| Old 3DS mode | `citra_is_new_3ds` = Old 3DS: 2 JITs, 128 MiB FCRAM saved in states, no 804 MHz mode | N3DS-only/enhanced games refuse or lose features |
| Resolution 1x, accurate mul off, texture filter none, HLE audio | Section 3.3-3.4 | Minor accuracy loss (mul) in a few games |
| Async shader compilation | Missing geometry for a few frames instead of a stall (`vk_rasterizer.cpp:470`) | Visual pops in the first minutes |
| Audio | Stretching is forced off in libretro (`core_settings.cpp:1132`): the app's pacing slows the sound; `enable_realtime_audio` as an experiment (`hle.cpp:468-474`) | Pitch drop when slow (as PSP today) |
| Mark as unplayable | New 3DS-only titles (`IsN3DSExclusive`, `core.cpp:427`); any game under ~50% after the knobs above, by title ID (`program_id`, `citra_libretro.cpp:407-409`), as a per-game preset like N64 CPU Timing Auto / PSP Frame Rate Auto | Honest expectations in the Library |

## 6. Open questions
1. No Jaguar measurement of a commercial game exists; everything above the fixed cost is an estimate until the first PS4 timing line.
2. What `std::thread::hardware_concurrency()` returns on the PS4 (sizes both worker pools), and whether the pools must be capped.
3. Which of the features Azahar reads from the physical GPU (`vk_instance.cpp:397-405`) the app's device can enable for it, and what each costs or breaks: geometryShader, custom border colour, extended dynamic state, timeline semaphores, index type uint8, robustness2 null descriptors, fragment shader interlock/barycentric, pipeline creation cache control (`research-3ds.md`, integration). Already there: logicOp (`Display.cpp:305, 341`), `VK_EXT_shader_stencil_export` when present (`:327-328`), fragmentStoresAndAtomics when the GPU has it (`:347`). lavapipe coped without several of them [measured].
4. Pipeline compile time on the PS4 for Azahar's pipelines (dynamic state, no MSAA): the PSP's 5-19 ms warm and 60-140 ms cold (God of War's 115 at start, 00.47) are the only references.
5. Whether `cpu_clock_percentage` below 100 gives real speed in the owner's games or only freezes (the Qt tooltip and the libretro help text point opposite ways, section 5).
6. JIT pool placement: static arrays (proven, resident) or run-time mapping (both JITs emit far calls, `block_of_code.h:86-100`; unproven on every firmware), and the size per JIT.
7. Whether dynarmic's `Unsafe_*` optimisation flags buy anything on Jaguar, and PGO's gain.
8. Which version to pin: 2126.2 (measured here) or 2127.0 (faster texture handling, FastInterp; alpha as of 2026-10-16).
